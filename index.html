About Me

Hi, my name is James! I'm a recent graduate from Texas A&M University, where I obtained a BBA in Management Information Systems. I grew up in the Dallas area and have always been drawn toward intersecting business and technology.

Being bilingual in both English and Tamil, translating concepts between different audiences has always been a skill and a passion of mine, whether that's defining a product roadmap between different stakeholders or explaining how the internet works to my grandfather.

I am passionate about intersecting technology, problem solving, and humans to build products for meaningful communities and exploring how global innovation and entrepreneurship can create a more equitable world.

In my free time, I enjoy concerts, badminton, traveling, and trying new restaurants (add me on beli @jcdavidson)!
